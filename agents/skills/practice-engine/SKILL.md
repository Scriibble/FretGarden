# PracticeEngine

## Mission

Optimize practice for durable learning, not merely session length or streak retention.

## Principles

- Favor frequent focused sessions over long unfocused sessions.
- Use Pomodoro-style timing as a support, not a punishment.
- Separate exposure, guided practice, retrieval, application, and review.
- Adjust difficulty gradually.
- Treat mistakes as learning signals.
- Avoid streak systems that punish necessary breaks.
- Make mastery thresholds explainable.

## Review workflow

1. Identify the skill being trained.
2. Define success and mastery.
3. Review scheduling and repetition logic.
4. Check whether difficulty responds appropriately.
5. Inspect feedback and rest behavior.
6. Identify gaming or frustration risks.
7. Recommend changes and tests.

## Required output

- Practice objective
- Current loop
- Learning-science concern
- Proposed change
- Data needed
- Edge cases
- Test plan
- User-facing explanation

## Guardrails

Do not introduce manipulative engagement mechanics. Do not claim scientific certainty without evidence. Keep algorithms understandable and debuggable.

## Graph Rules

- Every unit after Unit 1 requires the immediately preceding unit during Phase 1 mapping.
- Later authoring may replace a sequential edge with a more precise objective-level prerequisite, but it may not silently reorder source units.
- A mapped unit may be previewed, but it cannot be completed or produce curriculum evidence until implemented.
- Remediation returns to the lesson or objective that owns the missing capability; it does not erase prior history.

## Key Conceptual Dependencies

These dependencies remain required even when a teacher varies repertoire:

- Pulse before subdivision and syncopation.
- Clear fretting and release before sustained barre-chord endurance.
- Musical alphabet and whole/half steps before complete fretboard spelling.
- Intervals before triad construction.
- Triads and inversions before seventh chords and upper structures.
- Major-scale construction before diatonic harmony.
- Diatonic function before secondary dominants and modulation.
- Pentatonic phrasing before advanced modal improvisation.
- Stable meter and subdivision before odd, mixed, and layered meter.
- Motive and phrase construction before large-scale development.
- Basic aural matching before harmonic dictation and production-layer analysis.

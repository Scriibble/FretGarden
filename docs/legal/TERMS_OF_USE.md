# FretGarden Terms of Use

Effective date: July 15, 2026

FretGarden is an early-access guitar learning and practice application. By using the app or participating in testing, you agree to use it responsibly and understand the current product boundaries.

This document is a practical product terms draft, not legal advice. It should be reviewed before a broad public launch.

## Early-Access Status

FretGarden is actively being developed. Features may change, break, move, or be removed. The current app includes local-first practice tools, authored lesson content, and early account access.

Cloud progress sync, paid subscriptions, social features, user-generated content, and complete production persistence are not currently live.

## Accounts

You may create a FretGarden account using Supabase Auth. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in information secure. Do not use a display name that contains sensitive personal information or impersonates someone else.

Accounts do not currently sync lesson progress, drill history, or practice presets across devices.

## Local Progress

Practice data is currently stored in your browser. Clearing browser storage, switching devices, using private browsing, or changing browsers may remove or hide local progress.

FretGarden should not be treated as the only copy of important practice records during early access.

## Educational Scope

FretGarden provides educational practice guidance, music-theory explanations, and self-check structures. It does not guarantee musical mastery, professional certification, audition readiness, therapeutic outcomes, or injury prevention.

Stop practicing if you experience sharp pain, numbness, burning, or persistent strain. Seek qualified instruction or medical advice when appropriate.

## Tester Feedback

If you participate in a test group, feedback is voluntary. Tester responses may be used to improve the product and to document accessibility, usability, and learning-quality evidence.

Do not submit private, confidential, or sensitive personal information in feedback forms.

## Acceptable Use

Do not use FretGarden to:

- attack, disrupt, or probe the service;
- attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems;
- submit malicious, unlawful, harassing, or abusive content;
- copy, scrape, or redistribute app content in a way that harms the project;
- misrepresent FretGarden as providing professional certification or medical advice.

License information must be finalized before broad public release.

## Availability

FretGarden may be unavailable during development, testing, deployments, or maintenance. Early-access use is provided without uptime guarantees.

## Changes

These terms may change as the product matures. Material changes should be reviewed before broader release.

# FretGarden Tester Consent And Session Guide

Effective date: July 15, 2026

This guide supports early test sessions for FretGarden. It is designed to gather release-readiness evidence without overstating what the product proves.

## Purpose

The test group helps evaluate:

- whether learners can understand the current lesson path;
- whether accessibility paths are usable;
- whether progress and evidence labels are clear;
- whether local-only progress boundaries are understood;
- whether the product feels ready for broader testing.

## Participation

Participation is voluntary. Testers may stop at any time. They may skip any survey question that is not required for routing or safety.

Do not collect sensitive personal information. Do not ask testers to disclose medical conditions, financial information, private account credentials, or anything unrelated to app testing.

## Current Product Boundaries

Before testing, tell participants:

- FretGarden is early-access software.
- Practice progress is stored locally in the browser.
- Accounts are real, but cloud progress sync is not live.
- The app does not record audio, evaluate tone automatically, or certify mastery.
- The goal is to observe usability and learning clarity, not to judge the tester's musical ability.

## Recommended Participant Groups

Use at least:

- one early guitar learner;
- one returning guitar learner;
- one guitar educator, curriculum author, or experienced musician reviewing instructional quality.

## Session Checklist

1. Confirm the tester understands the early-access boundary.
2. Ask the tester to use their normal device, browser, and accessibility tools if applicable.
3. Have the tester open the app, lessons, practice tools, and progress surfaces.
4. Ask them to complete a short lesson/practice task without coaching.
5. Ask them to explain what the app claimed about their progress.
6. Record blockers, confusion, accessibility barriers, and suggested wording changes.
7. Ask the tester to submit the survey.

## Evidence Handling

Summaries may be used in release-readiness records. Direct quotes should be anonymized unless the tester agrees to attribution.

Any blocker should be resolved before final release or explicitly accepted with an owner, reason, and expiration.
